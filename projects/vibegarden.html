01 OVERVIEW

A garden for your thoughts.

VibeGarden is an interactive virtual garden where users can plant different crops and attach notes to capture thoughts, ideas, and memories.

I wanted to create something simple and relaxing that combined creativity with interactive web development. Instead of writing down an idea in a traditional notes app, VibeGarden turns those thoughts into something visual.

02 THE CONCEPT

Turning ideas into a garden.

The concept behind VibeGarden was to make a digital space where thoughts could grow alongside a visual garden.

Users can choose different plants, place them in their garden, and add personal notes. The garden becomes a visual representation of the things they want to remember or explore.
