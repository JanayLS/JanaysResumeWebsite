OVERVIEW

A survival game built around choices.

Plague Village is an interactive survival-based game set during a plague outbreak. The player takes on the role of a traveling plague doctor who arrives in a dying medieval village.

Rats spread disease through the streets, villagers fall ill, and time is limited. Players must gather ingredients, brew potions, battle rats, and decide who to treat while managing their resources and making choices that affect the outcome.

GAMEPLAY

Every decision matters.

Players begin each playthrough with randomized inventory items and money. From there, they must manage their resources and choose how to spend their limited Action Tokens throughout the day.

✦

Explore

Explore the forest and collect randomized ingredients for potion brewing.

✦

Brew

Use collected ingredients to create potions with different levels of effectiveness.

✦

Heal

Treat villagers using healing tonics, fever suppressants, and special items.

✦

Fight

Battle rats while managing the risk of increasing the Doctor's infection.

✦

Pray

Visit the chapel for chance-based prayer events that can help the village.

✦

Decide

Make choices that affect villagers, resources, and the final outcome.

THE GAME SYSTEM

20 days.
10 action tokens.

Players have a limited amount of time and actions to complete their objectives. When Action Tokens run out, the player returns to the village for an End of Day report.

20 Days
10 Action Tokens / Day
100% Doctor Infection Limit

WHAT I LEARNED

Building the game taught us as much as playing it.

The development process came with challenges including scheduling conflicts, communication gaps, defining the project's scope, and balancing ambitious ideas with a realistic project size.

The team ultimately identified the importance of clearly establishing roles, maintaining regular communication, defining scope early, and focusing on a strong minimum viable product.
