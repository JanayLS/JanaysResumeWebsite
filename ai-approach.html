MY APPROACH TO TECHNOLOGY

Use the tool.
Keep the thinking.

I see AI as both a learning tool and a productivity tool. It can help me understand something new, work through a problem, or explore an idea. But the goal is still to understand the work and make thoughtful decisions.

01

THE BIG IDEA

Helpful doesn't mean hands-off.

AI can make knowledge and new approaches easier to access, but its answers are not automatically right. I think the value comes from how you use it: asking questions, considering the response, and deciding what actually makes sense for the task.

For me, using AI well means staying involved in the process. It can support my work, but it shouldn't replace my curiosity, critical thinking, or responsibility for the final result.

TWO SIDES OF THE SAME TOOL

Learn more. Build better.

The way I use AI depends on what the project—or the problem—needs.

↗

01 / LEARNING

Use it to understand.

When I come across something unfamiliar, AI can help explain a concept, break down code, or show another way to think about a problem. I can ask follow-up questions instead of stopping at an answer I don't understand.

A useful approach

"Can you explain why this works and walk me through it step by step?"

{ }

02 / PRODUCTIVITY

Use it to move forward.

AI can help brainstorm ideas, compare possible solutions, troubleshoot errors, or get a first draft started. That can save time and give me more room to focus on the choices that need my attention.

A useful approach

"What are a few ways to solve this, and what are the trade-offs?"

02

MY WORKFLOW

Ask. Evaluate. Test. Learn.

I don't use one approach for every situation. Sometimes I need an explanation, sometimes I need help finding the source of an error, and sometimes I want to explore how to build a feature. The important part is staying engaged instead of treating an AI response as the final word.

01

Ask

Describe the goal, context, or problem clearly.

02

Evaluate

Review the answer and question anything unclear or questionable.

03

Test

Check whether the suggestion works in the actual project.

04

Learn

Take away the understanding, not just the immediate solution.

✦

RESPONSIBLE USE

The answer still needs human judgment.

AI can be confidently wrong, miss important context, or suggest code that doesn't fit a project's needs. That's why I believe developers should review suggestions, test behavior, think about security and reliability, and be honest about what they understand.

Using AI doesn't remove responsibility from the person using it. The developer still needs to decide what to keep, what to change, and whether the final result is appropriate for the task.

STAY CURIOUS

Technology keeps changing.
Learning should too.

I want to keep learning how to use new tools thoughtfully—not just to get things done faster, but to keep growing as a developer.
